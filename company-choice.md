# Company Choice

## Selected Company

I choose **Brasaland**.

Brasaland is the best fit for me because the operational problems are concrete and measurable across a real multi-country setup: 14 locations, two markets (Colombia and Florida), 115 employees, and about 6 million USD in annual revenue. The context shows clear pain points I want to solve with engineering: ingredient orders still managed by WhatsApp, disconnected POS systems, no centralized API, and leadership decisions made from delayed PDF reports. I also find the customer side compelling because the current website does not support online ordering, the app rating is only 2.8, and the physical loyalty card system has low adoption and poor data capture. This combination of operations, data, and customer experience creates a strong environment for building practical AI and automation that can produce visible business impact quickly.

## Departments I Find Most Interesting

1. **Restaurant Operations**: I am interested in building real-time visibility for daily performance by location and reducing stockouts/overstock with smarter ingredient planning.
2. **Technology**: This area is foundational because Brasaland currently lacks an internal API and telemetry; solving this unlocks better products for operations, marketing, and executive reporting.
3. **Executive Direction**: I am interested in the decision-support side, especially transforming delayed manual reports into natural-language analytics and automated weekly insights.

## Milestone Challenge I Most Want to Build

The challenge I am most excited about is the **AI assistant for executive and operational decision-making**, connected to a centralized API and near real-time telemetry. I want to make it possible to ask business questions such as weekly sales by market, top locations by average ticket, and anomaly alerts during opening hours without waiting for manual reports.

## My AI Agent Idea

I would build an **Operations + Executive Insight Agent** for Brasaland. The agent would continuously read sales transactions, location opening schedules, POS telemetry events, menu/catalog data, and basic inventory snapshots by store. It would detect operational anomalies (for example, no sales during open hours, sudden ticket drops, or unusual product mix), generate short summaries for managers, and trigger actions such as sending an alert to operations supervisors or creating a Monday 7:00 AM executive report draft. The same agent would support natural-language questions from leadership (for example, "How much did we sell this week in Florida vs Medellin?") and return answers in both COP and USD with location-level breakdowns.
